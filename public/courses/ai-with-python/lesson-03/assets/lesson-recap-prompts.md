用于第三课封面后的“前情提要”页，可切换查看第一、二课剧情。第三课封面仅展示 `diary-defense-cover.png`；前两课的四格回顾图使用内置 imagegen 生成，角色、画风参考第三课原图。

剧情依据：第一课守门模型的已知身份档案、攻击／防御／血量与新来客预测；第二课取回情报网页、提取并配对恶魔／地点／弱点、准备防御。

## 第一课

素材：`lesson-01-recap.png`

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: classroom projected four-panel story recap. Create a NEW 16:9 landscape image, four equally sized wide panels in a clean 2x2 grid separated by thin cream gutters. Reference image is STYLE AND CHARACTER REFERENCE ONLY, do not repeat its plot or modify it. Match its polished detailed warm storybook anime, cobalt blue and gold castle world, messy brown-haired young knight in blue cloak with gold castle emblem, silver/gold shoulder armor and brown gloves; yellow small two-eyed silver-goggled companion in blue denim overalls (Xiaopai) and blue/gold mechanical owl. Keep faces and costumes consistent across the four panels. Full bleed, no title area. No readable text, no letters, numbers, captions or speech bubbles: only abstract record strokes and clear simple pictograms. All essential action and faces fully inside each panel. Gentle school age fantasy, no gore or injury. Each panel must be a clear narrative moment; readable when projected. Primary request: recap LESSON ONE, train an AI castle gatekeeper to predict warriors versus demons from labelled examples. Story in left-right/top-bottom order. TOP LEFT: castle gate faces a confusing crowd: friendly blue-cloaked warriors and purple horned demons arriving together, young hero and Xiaopai concerned at guard station, no fighting. TOP RIGHT: inside warm training room, young hero and Xiaopai compare several labelled training archive cards: warrior portrait marked by blue shield icon, demon portrait marked by purple horn icon, three horizontal graphical meters on EACH card representing attack, defense and health; hero arranges examples beside a friendly blue-and-gold robot gatekeeper and a computer. There are BOTH warriors and demons with varied meter lengths, never portray a single visual trait as an absolute rule. BOTTOM LEFT: after training, friendly robot gatekeeper at doorway receives ONE NEW visitor's archive card with three graphical meters, a blue-cloaked warrior waiting outside, screen showing candidate warrior and demon portrait icons while it estimates identity. No perfect accuracy halo, no image scanning eyes: it uses the numeric archive card. BOTTOM RIGHT: clear successful observed result: the same blue gold robot has opened the gate for the friendly blue-cloaked warrior, purple horned demon waits blocked outside a closed side barrier, young hero and Xiaopai pleased on battlement. This is the observed classroom success, not a claim the model is always correct.
```
