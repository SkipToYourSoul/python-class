## 最终提示词

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: panoramic 3:1 classroom comic banner for lesson 5's second practice, shown below Python code.
Primary request: Create a new moment in the same fantasy story: the brown-haired young knight and the small yellow assistant compare three different views of the SAME six patrol observations, realise a demon's speed changes during the day, and confidently plan further scouting before the final battle.
Input images: image 1 speed-clue.png is a character and illustration-style reference only, not an edit target. image 2 fieldwork.png is a reference for the same fiery horned demon and castle landscape.
Scene: a sunlit stone castle observation room, a wooden planning table, a distant castle through an arch. Same warm high-quality anime illustration with polished painterly fantasy details.
Subjects: the same brown-haired knight, silver-and-gold armour, bright blue scarf/cape, expressive amber eyes, at left; the same small yellow two-eyed goggled assistant with blue overalls at right. The knight thoughtfully smiles and points to the evidence, holding a folded scouting map. Assistant confidently places one new observation pin on the map. No extra characters.
Key evidence: a LARGE cream evidence board at the centre with three clear simple chart sketches side by side: scatter dots, a line with six marked dots, and six separated blue bars. All show the same low-rise-rise-fall-fall-rise pattern, third value highest, fifth lowest; no written numbers or labels. These are symbolic story props, real charts and conclusions are separately rendered on the web page. Small flame demon sketch and a brass clock on the table link the charts to patrol observations.
Composition: wide panoramic 3:1, faces, hands, map and three charts stay in the central 80% width and middle 65% height so the banner can crop slightly. Clear expressions and evidence-board silhouettes. Mood: purposeful discovery and increased confidence, preparations ongoing, no combat.
Text: absolutely no words, speech balloons, letters, logos or watermark.
Avoid: histogram bars touching, three contradictory trends, battle victory, other monster species, extreme close-up, clutter hiding the charts.
